# AI-11 recall report (2026-10-26)

**Decision: `local/BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` with 180-word chunks: recall@5 80% (32/40). Vector dimension: 384.**

**Chosen by Ziad Abdullah (AI lead), not by the decision rule.** The rule alone picked `minilm-l6` with 120-word chunks. Why: All 9 model and chunk-size combinations are within noise of each other (30-35 of 40). bge-small keeps AI-10's reviewed 180-word chunks, has 384 dimensions and a 512-token window, and is stronger than MiniLM on public retrieval benchmarks.

What the rule found:
- minilm-l6: 120 words beats 180 by 2 questions, so it uses 120
- best recall@5: 33/40 questions; within 1 of it: minilm-l6, bge-small, bge-base; the tie goes to fewer dimensions, then faster indexing: minilm-l6 (384 dimensions)

## How to read this

- With 40 questions, one question is 2.5 points. Differences under about 5 points are noise, which is why the decision rule needs a model to lead by more than 1 question and another chunk size to lead 180 by 2.
- The questions were written by the AI lead's assistant from the pages, as paraphrases. They may still be easier than real users' queries; review questions.yaml before relying on small differences.
- Every query searches its whole domain: in marketing, 6 pages (including a synthetic one) compete; in technology/ai, 3.
- all-MiniLM-L6-v2 reads at most 256 tokens, so the end of a long chunk (250 words is about 330 tokens) does not count toward its vector.
